Beyond the Prompt: Building Reliable Agentic Systems

Most agent failures are not failures of reasoning. We traced 312 failed runs and found that 54% broke at the boundary between a model and a tool. Enforcing four contracts at those boundaries moved long tasks from unreliable to dependable.

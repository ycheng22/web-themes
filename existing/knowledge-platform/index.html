Beyond the Prompt: Building Reliable Agentic Systems

Most agent failures are not failures of reasoning. We traced 312 failed runs and found 54% broke at the boundary between a model and a tool. Four contracts at those boundaries raised success on 30-step tasks from 41% to 83%.

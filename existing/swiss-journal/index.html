Beyond the Prompt: Building Reliable Agentic Systems

Most agent failures are not failures of reasoning. We traced 312 failed runs and found that 54% broke at the boundary between a model and a tool. This article describes the runtime, the evaluation harness and the four contracts that made agents dependable enough to leave unattended.

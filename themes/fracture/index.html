How we think

Manifesto

The grid is a starting point

We draw the orthogonal plan first, then break it where the site, the sun or the program asks.

Every angle earns its place

A tilted wall must bring light, frame a view or move people. Otherwise it goes back to straight.

Built, not rendered

Every fracture is detailed so it can be built by a normal contractor, on budget.

About and now

Studio

Oduya Architects is eleven people in Lagos and Lisbon. Nia Oduya founded the studio in 2014 after years of building very straight schools and wanting, once, to bend a wall.

We still build many straight walls. We just argue about each one.

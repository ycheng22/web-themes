About and now

I started in a print studio, kerning headlines by hand, and moved to the browser when I realised the page could respond. Ten years later I still care about the same things: rhythm, contrast and the space between letters, now also between states.

Most of my work sits where a design file turns into a product. I build the systems that keep the two in step, then I use them to make things people enjoy using.

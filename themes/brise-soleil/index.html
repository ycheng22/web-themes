About and now

Oduya Architects is eleven people in Lagos and Lisbon. We learned from the tropical modernists who built for heat before air conditioning existed, and we still draw every sun path by hand.

Founded in 2014 by Nia Oduya after a decade of designing schools and clinics in West Africa.

The studio

Oduya Architects is a studio of eleven in Lagos and Lisbon. We believe ornament is not decoration but care made visible: a handrail you want to touch, a ceiling you look up at.

We work with craftspeople from the first sketch: metalworkers, terrazzo makers and glass artists.

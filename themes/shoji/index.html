Studioabout and now

Oduya Architects is eleven people working between Lagos and Lisbon, with a long collaboration with joiners in Kyoto. Nia Oduya founded the studio in 2014 after years designing libraries and schools.

We take few projects and stay with each from first sketch to first winter.

About

Oduya Architects is eleven people in Lagos and Lisbon, working with tile cutters in Fez and plasterers in Marrakesh. Nia Oduya founded the studio in 2014 after years designing schools for hot climates.

We design for shade first: courtyards, deep arcades and water that cools the air before the building needs to.
